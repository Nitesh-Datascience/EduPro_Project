# Executive summary for education stakeholders

EduPro's supplied data contains 3,000 learners, 60 courses, and 10,000 enrollments. A reproducible analysis finds two broad learner patterns: 2,533 early-stage learners who enrolled in about 1.5 courses across 1.5 categories on average, and 467 cross-domain explorers who enrolled in about 13.1 courses across 8.5 categories. The two-cluster solution has a silhouette score of 0.428; a second clustering method shows strong agreement on a sample. These groups describe past enrollment behavior and should not label learner capability.

The dashboard lets staff inspect overall enrollment, compare segments, select a learner, and see unseen courses ranked by category fit, level fit, segment popularity, and course rating. It offers a transparent starting point for improving course discovery. For early-stage learners, a short next-step path may reduce choice overload; explorers can be shown a wider range of related topics.

The workbook cannot show whether these recommendations improve completion, engagement, or retention. A pilot should record what was displayed and what learners subsequently chose and completed, then compare outcomes with the existing system through a controlled trial. Review accessibility, privacy, and fairness before broad rollout. Do not publish the supplied names and email addresses in a public repository.
